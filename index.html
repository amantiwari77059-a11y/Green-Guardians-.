🌿 About Us

Why We Exist

Our campus generates hundreds of kilograms of waste every single day — most of it unsegregated, ending up in landfills that harm our environment and community. We refuse to accept that as the norm.

Green Guardians was founded by students who believe every campus has the power to lead by example. Our mission is to build a cleaner, more sustainable NIELIT campus through waste segregation, recycling, composting, and student-led awareness initiatives — creating habits that last a lifetime.

♻️ Our Activities

What We Do

From daily drives to monthly campaigns — every action counts towards a cleaner campus.

🗑️

Waste Segregation Drives

Regular wet, dry, and e-waste segregation drives across hostels, departments, and common areas to ensure waste reaches the right bins.

🌱

Composting Pit & Campus Garden

We convert organic waste into compost that nourishes our campus garden — a living example of a closed-loop waste system.

💻

E-Waste Collection Drives

Collecting old electronics, batteries, and cables for responsible recycling — keeping hazardous materials out of landfills.

🚫🥤

Plastic-Free Campus Initiative

Distributing cloth bags, promoting reusable bottles, and working with the canteen to eliminate single-use plastic on campus.

✂️

Upcycling Workshops

Creative hands-on sessions that teach students how to transform waste materials into useful, beautiful products.

🏆

Adopt a Zone Competition

Hostels and departments compete to maintain the cleanest zones — gamifying cleanliness to build lasting habits and healthy rivalry.

📢

Awareness Campaigns

Posters, social media campaigns, flash mobs, and street plays that spread the message of sustainability across the campus community.
